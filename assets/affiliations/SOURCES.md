# Affiliation logo sources

The homepage uses local WebP derivatives normalized to a 256 × 256 square canvas and displayed at 64 × 64 pixels. Transparent backgrounds are retained where the source mark permits them.

- Amazon: user-selected black-and-orange [Amazon icon vector](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazon_icon.svg)
- Purdue University: user-selected black-and-gold University Seal treatment, checked against Purdue's [official brand guidelines](https://marcom.purdue.edu/app/uploads/2022/02/brand-strategy-guideline.pdf)
- TikTok: colorful square note icon from the [official TikTok developer logo pack](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/getting-started-design-guidelines)
- Xi'an Jiaotong University: round university seal from the [XJTU visual identity portal](https://vi.xjtu.edu.cn/)
- UC Berkeley: [UC Berkeley seal vector](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Seal_of_University_of_California,_Berkeley.svg), with usage guidance from [Berkeley Brand](https://brand.berkeley.edu/visual-identity/icons-secondary-marks/uc-berkeley-seal/)
- National University of Singapore: shield extracted from the full-color NUS identity artwork; see the [NUS identity guidelines](https://nus.edu.sg/identity/guidelines/logo-guideline)

All names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
